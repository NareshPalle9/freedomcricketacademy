About Us

Where talent meets opportunity

Freedom Cricket Academy nurtures young talent through expert coaching, modern facilities and a deep love for the game. From first-time holders of the bat to state-level aspirants, we help every player reach their potential.

  • BCCI-certified coaching staff
  • Floodlit turf & practice nets
  • Fitness, nutrition & mental conditioning
  • Regular tournaments and match exposure
